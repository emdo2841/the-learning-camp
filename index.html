Why These Programs Matter

Evidence-based impact: TaRL methodology has been proven effective across multiple countries, consistently improving learning outcomes for students who have fallen behind

Sustainable model: By training teachers to mentor their peers, we create a self-sustaining ecosystem of professional development that continues long after initial implementation.
